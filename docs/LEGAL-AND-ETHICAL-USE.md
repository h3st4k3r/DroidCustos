# Legal and Ethical Use

Author: h3st4k3r  
Applies to: DroidCustos 0.3.2

## Authorized use only

Use DroidCustos only when the examination is lawful, documented and within the approved scope. The operator is responsible for authorization, privacy, retention, disclosure and handling requirements.

## Explicit consent requirement for integrated MVT tools

The standard DroidCustos workflow integrates MVT and AndroidQF. Both are distributed under the MVT License 1.1, which includes a consensual-use restriction requiring explicit consent from the person whose data is extracted or analyzed.

This is a software-license condition in addition to any legal authority. A warrant, employer ownership, acceptable-use policy or statutory power does not by itself modify the upstream license.

Law-enforcement and public-sector operators should use the integrated workflow only in cooperative, victim-support or other consent-based examinations where the data owner gives explicit consent and local procedure permits it.

## Data minimization

Before acquisition, decide whether the case requires:

- SMS or broad backup attempts.
- All APKs or only non-system APKs.
- Browser artifacts.
- Chat and notification artifacts.
- Network-state artifacts.
- User-file hashes.
- Copies of shared personal files.
- Use of pre-existing root access.

Collect only what is necessary and authorized.

## Sensitive populations

Mobile acquisitions can expose sources, contacts, location, health information, political activity, legal communications and personal relationships. Apply enhanced controls when assisting journalists, human-rights defenders, minors, victims of abuse or other at-risk persons.

## Evidence handling

- Use encrypted storage.
- Restrict access by role.
- Preserve original evidence separately from working copies.
- Keep private signing keys separate.
- Avoid consumer cloud synchronization.
- Do not upload evidence to public analysis services.
- Document every transfer and disclosure.
- Follow applicable retention and destruction rules.

## No admissibility representation

DroidCustos records custody and integrity metadata, but the project does not declare that its output is admissible in any jurisdiction. Admissibility depends on law, procedure, validation, analyst competence, acquisition conditions and independent verification.

## No covert or adversarial use

Do not use DroidCustos or its integrated tools for covert extraction, surveillance, stalking, unauthorized employee monitoring, intimate-partner abuse, credential theft or privacy invasion.
